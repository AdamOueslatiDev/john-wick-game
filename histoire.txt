After the death of his wife Helen, John Wick receives a puppy named Daisy
to help him cope with his grief.  
When criminals break into his home, they steal his car and kidnap
Daisy instead of killing her,forcing John back into the 
assassin underworld with a new goal: 
rescue rather than pure revenge. 
As John fights through New York’s criminal network, 
he discovers that Helen was once connected to this 
hidden world and helped 
him escape it, making his return unavoidable. 
The strict rules of the assassin society still 
govern every encounter, with safe 
zones, contracts, and consequences shaping the 
journey. In the final confrontation, John chooses 
to dismantle the criminal 
organization instead of simply killing its leader, 
breaking the cycle of violence. The story ends with 
John walking away with Daisy, 
leaving the underworld behind once again, but with 
the suggestion that its shadow will always follow him.
